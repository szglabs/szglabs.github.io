Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our engineering and AI services

1. What engineering and AI services does SZG Labs provide?

SZG Labs provides senior-level engineering services across artificial intelligence (AI) systems, machine learning, LLM integration, DevOps consulting, custom software development, high-throughput data pipelines, and enterprise systems integration (ERP/EDI/Cloud).

2. How do you integrate AI and LLMs into existing business systems?

We architect secure, production-grade AI solutions tailored to your infrastructure. This includes private Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, vector database setup (pgvector, OpenSearch), LLM orchestration via AWS Bedrock or OpenAI/Anthropic APIs, and autonomous agent workflows connected to your ERP, CRM, or data warehouse.

3. Who actually works on client engagements?

Every engagement at SZG Labs is staffed 100% with senior engineers who have built, deployed, and scaled production systems. We don't use junior engineers, account intermediaries, or offshore handoffs — you work directly with technical practitioners.

4. What technologies and platforms does SZG Labs specialize in?

Our core technical competencies include Cloud & DevOps (AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker, GitHub Actions), AI & Data (Python, pgvector, AWS Bedrock, ETL pipelines, PostgreSQL), Software (Go, Python, TypeScript, Node.js), and Enterprise Integrations (Odoo ERP v17–19, EDI ANSI X12 850/856/810, REST/GraphQL APIs).

5. How are client engagements structured?

We work under flexible arrangements including project-based fixed deliverables, dedicated senior engineering pods (staff augmentation), and retainer-based architecture & advisory support. All projects start with a technical deep-dive consultation to map requirements, timelines, and measurable success criteria.

6. How does Agentic AI automation compare to hiring manual data entry staff?

Agentic AI workflows built by SZG Labs typically replace repetitive manual processes such as order entry, inventory reconciliation, and document routing. A single automated pipeline can handle work that would otherwise require one or more full-time data entry clerks at $40,000–$60,000 per year each, while operating 24/7 without errors, sick days, or onboarding time. Clients typically see full ROI within 2–4 months of deployment.

7. Does SZG Labs specialize in Odoo ERP?

Yes. SZG Labs specializes in Odoo ERP architecture, implementation, and customization across versions 17 through 19. We handle complex multi-company configurations, custom module development, inventory and manufacturing workflows, and integrations with third-party systems including eCommerce platforms, warehouse management systems, and EDI trading partners.
