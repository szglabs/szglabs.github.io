Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

SZG Labs, LLC ("SZG Labs," "we," "our," or "us"), a Nevada limited liability company, is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients, prospective customers, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your information across our website (szglabs.com) and our commercial client operations.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you or automatically when you interact with our Site and services:

  • Contact & Inquiry Details: Full name, corporate email address, phone number, company name, and project requirements submitted via our contact forms or email inquiries.
  • Billing & Contract Information: Corporate billing address, authorized signatory information, and transaction metadata required to process invoices and execute Statements of Work (SOWs).
  • Technical & Analytical Telemetry: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, device telemetry, and page interaction timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal and corporate information for legitimate business purposes:

  • To evaluate project scopes, respond to technical inquiries, and provide custom consulting estimates.
  • To draft, execute, and manage commercial service agreements and SOWs via digital contracting platforms.
  • To generate invoices, process card/ACH payments, and maintain compliant business accounting records.
  • To optimize website performance, improve user experience, and ensure infrastructure security.
  • To comply with statutory tax, legal, and regulatory obligations.

3. AI Architecture & Zero Model Training Policy

When engineering bespoke Agentic AI workflows, LLM pipelines, or automated ETL middleware for our clients, SZG Labs enforces a strict Zero Data Retention and Zero Model Training standard:

  • Client data, confidential records, proprietary documents, and prompts are processed strictly through enterprise-tier API endpoints with zero training rights.
  • Client data is never used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve public or foundation artificial intelligence models.
  • Data processed by autonomous agent workflows is held ephemerally during pipeline execution and disposed of according to the client's Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

4. Authorized Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted enterprise service providers to operate our business infrastructure. These providers process information strictly on our behalf under confidentiality agreements:

  • Invoicing & Accounting: Zoho Corporation (Zoho Books / Zoho Payments for PCI-compliant billing and client portal).
  • Digital Contracting: Common Paper, Inc. (Standardized SOWs, DPAs, and electronic signatures).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • Source Code & CI/CD: GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft Corporation.
  • Inquiry Form Processing: Formspree, Inc.

A full, current list of authorized processors is available on our Approved Subprocessors Page.

5. Cookies and Web Analytics

We use standard first-party and third-party cookies and tracking technologies (such as Google Analytics) to analyze aggregate Site traffic and user interaction patterns. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though some Site features may operate with reduced functionality.

6. Data Sharing & Zero-Sale Commitment

SZG Labs does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes. We disclose information only to authorized service providers, when executing client contracts, or when required by lawful subpoena, court order, or applicable government regulation.

7. State-Specific Privacy Rights (Nevada & California)

  • Nevada Residents (NRS 603A): Nevada residents have the right to opt out of the sale of covered personal information. Although SZG Labs does not sell personal information, you may submit a formal verification request by emailing compliance@szglabs.com.
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): If applicable, California residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information held by SZG Labs without discrimination.

8. Data Security & Storage

We maintain multi-layered administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of personal data. Detailed technical controls are documented on our Security Page.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our service offerings, legal requirements, or technical architecture. Material changes will be indicated by revising the Effective Date at the top of this page.
